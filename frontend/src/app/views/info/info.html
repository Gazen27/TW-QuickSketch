ABOUT QUICKSKETCH

QUICKSKETCH is where creativity meets competition.

A drawing and guessing game where imagination is your greatest tool. Whether you're an aspiring artist or simply love a good challenge, QUICKSKETCH brings people together through creativity, intuition, and a little friendly competition. Draw, guess, explore, and climb the leaderboards. Test your drawing skills, challenge your ability to recognize even the trickiest sketch, discover creations from other players, and earn your place among the best.

QUICKSKETCH was created as an academic project back in 2026 for the Web Technologies course at the University of Naples Federico II (UNINA).

STATS AND SCORES

Your profile keeps track of your performance and progress throughout your QUICKSKETCH journey.

Guessed Score represents the points earned by correctly guessing drawings. The more words you guess correctly, the higher your score becomes. Each correctly guessed sketch earns you +1 point.

Drawing Score represents the points earned by creating drawings that other players successfully guess. The better your drawings communicate the intended word, the more opportunities you have to earn points. Each drawing that is correctly guessed by at least one player earns you +1 point.

Your statistics help you track your progress over time and compare your performance with other players through the leaderboards. Every point contributes to your ranking, so keep drawing, guessing, and improving!
